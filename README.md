# CodeInSight
As codebases evolve, developers’ mental models fall out of sync. CodeInSight brings your understanding in sight - and gives you real insight - by comparing what you think the system does with how it actually works.  Visual diffs, structural diagrams, and guided planning help teams stay aligned, onboard faster, and ship with confidence.

## Skills

- **[reality-check](skills/reality-check/SKILL.md)** — describe your mental model of a codebase area (architecture, deployment, or data flow), and get back a Markdown report with a plain diagram of your model next to a red/amber/green-graded diagram of reality.
